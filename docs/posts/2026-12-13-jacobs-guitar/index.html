Jacob’s Guitar

The same chords, redistributed across six strings and five.

A regular classical guitar has six strings, usually tuned E–A–D–G–B–E from lowest to highest. Jacob’s guitar has five: D–A–E–A–D. The two instruments cover a similar tonal range, but they distribute the notes differently.

Choose a major or minor chord below to compare one playable voicing on each instrument. Then select any played note to trace the same pitch across both fretboards.
