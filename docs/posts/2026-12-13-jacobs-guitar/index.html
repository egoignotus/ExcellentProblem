Jacob’s Guitar

The same chords, redistributed across six strings and five.
music
Author

Aleksandra

Published

December 13, 2026

A regular classical guitar has six strings, usually tuned E–A–D–G–B–E from lowest to highest. Jacob’s guitar has five: D–A–E–A–D. The two instruments cover a similar tonal range, but they distribute the notes differently.

Here are D major (D–F♯–A) and E major (E–G♯–B) on both instruments. Each diagram shows one fixed voicing, with strings ordered from lowest to highest, left to right.

On the five-string guitar, these two voicings use the same shape two frets apart: 0–0–2–0–0 becomes 2–2–4–2–2. For E major, a barre at the second fret takes the place of the open strings in D major.
